# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Read it there for the full text. It applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where people speak for the project.

## In short

- Be kind and respectful to everyone.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

These are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub: [@irfanfaraaz](https://github.com/irfanfaraaz). Reports are handled in confidence.

The maintainer may remove comments, issues or pull requests, and may ban people who break this code.
